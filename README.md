# LUKHΛS AI

**Exploring structured, evidence-aware and human-governed artificial intelligence.**

**LUKHΛS** stands for **Logical Unified Knowledge Hyper-Adaptable System**. It is a research and engineering programme investigating symbolic representations, modular reasoning, memory, evidence trails and human authority in AI systems.

Our goals are to make AI more adaptable, more transparent and auditable, and less likely to cause harm. These are **design objectives**, not claims that every prototype has achieved them.

## Evidence before claims

A research proposal, working script, integrated service and validated capability are not the same thing. We distinguish **research**, **implemented code**, **tested behavior** and **deployment status**. A repository's existence is not proof of production readiness, guaranteed safety or quantum or conscious capabilities.

Where code and documentation disagree, reproducible tests and specific source revisions matter more than aspirational language. We welcome precise bug reports, independent evaluation and falsifiable research questions.

The work is independently developed. Use of third-party AI tools does not imply official endorsement, sponsorship or partnership.
